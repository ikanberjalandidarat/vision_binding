Recording metadata passed. The eight authored environments have two complete real-Minecraft routes each: 16 trajectories and 3,257 recorded frames. All routes start on tick 0, save a frame every eight ticks, have distinct recorded image hashes within each route, and reach all declared waypoints. Build start positions, pose bounds, action-clock alignment, and specification hashes are consistent. The hill routes span roughly eight blocks vertically.

Two source-code hashes occur across the collection jobs: 00/03/04/07 share one, and 01/02/05/06 share the other. The recorded capture settings and each environment's specification digest pass the checks.

Route coverage

Positions after the initial 72-tick camera scan were rounded down to Minecraft block cells. “Shared cells” compares the two routes in the same world. Jaccard is intersection divided by union of their visited cells. “Different view” is the fraction of shared cells where the nearest sampled headings across routes differ by at least 90°. These are descriptive measures, not preregistered thresholds or evidence of neural place/grid cells.

Interpretation: the two maze routes and the mine routes mostly traverse the same cells from similar headings. A cross-route position decoder in those scenes could exploit repeated views; success alone would not establish an allocentric map. The representative forest frames include the pond but often show the perimeter fence, and the mine recordings are visually repetitive. The eight categories are present, but this is an exploratory dataset with one authored world per category, not a generalization benchmark across worlds within each category. The path coverage is also not adequate for a strong gridness claim.

Decision

Keep v2 intact as the first standardized eight-scene pilot and proceed with an explicitly exploratory frozen-video location probe. Do not label any model unit a place or grid cell from this dataset. A later route/viewpoint expansion should add reverse or independently routed traversals and more two-dimensional revisits; it should be versioned separately rather than replacing these recordings.

The earlier extractor expected one top-level sequences.json. The new spatial_video_dataset.py adapter reads the eight per-environment files and supplies a fixed evaluation split; it passed local metadata and synthetic fixture checks, the reported Oscar raw-frame gate, a one-clip pretrained-model smoke check (job 7982755), and full extraction of 188 clips (job 7982818). Spatial analysis remains unrun. The encoder remains frozen; pose is for labels and audit, not model input. Dataset gate and model commands.
