Why PPO is stopping, and what the next experiment tests

The previous three methods achieved 0/56 present-target evaluation successes. Each collected only 246–248 training decisions. Greedy evaluation stopped immediately everywhere. This experiment tests an exploration failure; it cannot guarantee learning.

The reward calculation

With γ=0.99, per-step cost −0.01 and failure reward −1, a failed trajectory of any length T has discounted base return −1. Potential shaping r′=r+γΦ(s′)−Φ(s), with zero terminal potential, changes this to −1−Φ(s₀). Thus all failures from the same start have equal Monte Carlo returns. This explains zero GRPO group advantages, but does not establish that PPO is incorrect: its bootstrapped advantages can differ.

The recovery arm changes both termination and its associated reward: it tests that package, not an isolated reward coefficient. It uses simulator success information during training. No teacher action replaces the sampled action, and PPO replays the actual stored probability. No simulator coordinates enter the actor. The frozen Qwen encoder and supervised binding readout are unchanged.

Both arms use fixed 1-block privileged starts, 3 present tasks and 1 absent task per four-episode batch, a target of 1,500 decisions, a safety cap of 1,024 episodes, and 32 decisions per episode. Whole batches can overshoot the target. Check budget_met: reaching the safety cap is not budget completion. Starts do not become harder automatically. This first stage deliberately tests approach and stopping before returning to long navigation.

Measurements and decisions

  • Initial/final original-start evaluation: 16 validation tasks, each greedy and sampled.
  • Near-start probes: fixed training and validation panels at initialization, every 500 collected decisions, and completion. These are correlated development probes, not independent test scenes.
  • If near training performance remains poor, examine stop frequency, rewards and updates; do not scale to binding claims.
  • If near training improves but near validation does not, investigate perception/generalization.
  • If near validation improves but original-start performance does not, expand distance only after the easy stage works.
  • A provisional progression criterion is ≥80% present and absent success in both action modes at two distinct late near-validation checkpoints, replicated across seeds. Small panels require expanded confirmation, not a reliability claim.
